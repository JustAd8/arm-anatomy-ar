How BioScan AR Works

BioScan AR transforms your webcam into an augmented reality medical visualization scanner, tracking your arms live with deep anatomical precision.

  • Bone Structure: Full skeletal framework from the shoulder glenoid, humerus, elbow trochlea, radius and ulna down to the carpal rays.
  • Muscle System: Striated biceps brachii, triceps, deltoids, and forearm muscles with dynamic contraction bulging when flexing your elbow!
  • Vascular System: Superficial cephalic & basilic veins, median cubital vein (venipuncture site), and pulsating brachial and radial arteries with live rhythmic flow.
  • Interactive Inspector: Click or hover any anatomical component or select items from the Atlas to reveal medical descriptions, Latin terms, and clinical notes.
  • Demo Simulation Mode: If you do not have a webcam active or wish to study the 3D model effortlessly, toggle Camera Feed off to engage high-fidelity motion simulation!
